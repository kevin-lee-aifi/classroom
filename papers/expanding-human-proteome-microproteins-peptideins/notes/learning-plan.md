# Learning Plan: Expanding the human proteome with microproteins and peptideins

## Goal

Develop a solid understanding of microproteins and peptideins — what they are, how they're discovered, and their biological relevance — well enough to explain the key ideas to someone new to the field.

## Background Prerequisites

Before or while reading this paper, make sure you're comfortable with:

- [ ] Central dogma: transcription → translation
- [ ] Open reading frames (ORFs) and how they're annotated
- [ ] Ribosome profiling (Ribo-seq) basics
- [ ] Mass spectrometry for proteomics
